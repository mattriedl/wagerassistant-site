How a Lucky 31 Bet Works

A Lucky 31 is a full-cover bet on five selections across 31 bets: five singles, ten doubles, ten trebles, five four-folds and one five-fold. This lucky 31 calculator settles every combination and applies bonuses automatically.

Formula & Worked Example

The 31 bets are 5 singles + 10 doubles + 10 trebles + 5 fourfolds + 1 fivefold. At $1 a line that is $31 total. If all five win at 2.0 each the return is 5x2 + 10x4 + 10x8 + 5x16 + 1x32 = 10 + 40 + 80 + 80 + 32 = $242. As with a Lucky 15, many bookmakers add a single-winner bonus or an all-winners bonus.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Underestimating the 31-unit stake (62 units each-way).
  • Missing the bonus terms that make Lucky bets attractive.
  • Confusing a Lucky 31 with a Canadian/Super Yankee (no singles).
  • Assuming every book prices the bonus the same way.
