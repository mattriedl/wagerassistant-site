How Teasers Work

A teaser adjusts the point spread or total in your favor by a fixed number of points (6, 6.5, or 7) across all legs. In exchange, the payout is reduced compared to a standard parlay. All legs must win for the teaser to pay out.

Standard NFL Teaser Odds (6 pts): 2-team: −110, 3-team: +150, 4-team: +250, 5-team: +400.
Standard NBA Teaser Odds (4 pts): 2-team: −110, 3-team: +150, 4-team: +250.

The teaser calculator works out payouts when you move the spread in your favour across multiple legs. Choose your point adjustment (6, 6.5 or 7 points), add your legs and stake, and this NFL teaser calculator returns your combined teaser odds, payout and profit.

Teasers let you buy points on football and basketball spreads or totals in exchange for lower odds, but every leg must still win. Our teaser bet calculator shows whether the safer lines justify the reduced payout.

Pro Tips for Smarter Betting

How Teaser Bets Work

A teaser lets you move the point spread or total in your favor across two or more games in exchange for a lower payout. This teaser calculator applies the extra points to each leg and prices the adjusted parlay.

Formula & Worked Example

A standard NFL 6-point, 2-team teaser is typically priced around -120 (decimal 1.833). Move both spreads 6 points your way, and if both legs cover the new numbers a $110 stake returns 110 x 1.833 = $201.67, a profit of $91.67. The extra points dramatically raise your win chance, but because both legs must still cover, the payout is far lower than a standard parlay.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Teasing through key numbers like 3 and 7 incorrectly, wasting the point value.
  • Assuming extra points guarantee a win - both legs must still cover.
  • Forgetting teaser prices worsen as you add legs or points.
  • Treating a teaser like a straight parlay when comparing payouts.
