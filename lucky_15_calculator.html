How a Lucky 15 Bet Works

A Lucky 15 is a full-cover bet on four selections across 15 bets: four singles, six doubles, four trebles and one four-fold. This lucky 15 calculator settles every line and applies any bonuses, so even one winner returns money.

Formula & Worked Example

The 15 bets break down as 4 singles + 6 doubles + 4 trebles + 1 fourfold. At $1 per line that is $15 total. If all four win at 2.0 each: singles 4 x 2 = $8, doubles 6 x 4 = $24, trebles 4 x 8 = $32 and the fourfold 1 x 16 = $16, totalling $80. Many books add a bonus (often 10%) if all four win, or double the odds on a single winner.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overlooking the one-winner bonus some bookmakers apply.
  • Forgetting the 15-unit stake (30 units each-way).
  • Confusing a Lucky 15 (with singles) with a Yankee (without).
  • Assuming all books offer the same Lucky 15 bonus terms.
