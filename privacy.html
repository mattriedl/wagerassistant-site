Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is collected when you use wagerassistant.com and how it is used. The short version: the numbers you type into our calculators never leave your device, and we don’t ask for your name or create accounts.

Information you type into calculators

All calculations run in your web browser using JavaScript. The odds, stakes and other values you enter are not sent to our servers, are not stored by us and are not shared with anyone.

Information collected automatically

Analytics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used — for example, which calculators are popular, how visitors find us and what device types they use. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location (derived from your IP address), browser and device type, and referring site. We use these reports only in aggregate to improve the site. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Hosting and security (Cloudflare)

The site is delivered through Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as IP addresses and request details to deliver pages quickly and protect the site from abuse and automated attacks. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Advertising (BannerPromote)

Some pages show banner ads served by BannerPromote (bannerpromote.com). When an ad loads, your browser connects to the ad server, which receives standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the page you are viewing, in order to display the ad and count views and clicks.

Cookies

We don’t set our own advertising or tracking cookies. Cookies may be set by Google Analytics and by Cloudflare (for security). You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the calculators work without them.

Email

If you email us at contact@wagerassistant.com, we use your email address and message only to reply to you and to fix any problem you report. We don’t add you to a mailing list or sell your details.

Embedded calculators

If you use one of our calculators embedded on another website, the same rule applies: your inputs stay in your browser. The host website has its own privacy policy.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with the service providers described above, who process it to provide their services, or where required by law.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the UK and EU, or state privacy laws such as the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information about you and to object to certain processing. Because we don’t hold accounts or calculator inputs, most data about your visit is held by the providers above. To make a request, email contact@wagerassistant.com.

Children

This site is intended for adults of legal gambling age and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date below.
