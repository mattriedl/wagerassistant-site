How Margin Works

The bookmaker's margin (or overround) is the percentage above 100% when you sum all implied probabilities. True Probability = Implied Probability / Total Implied Probability. True Odds = 1 / True Probability. A lower margin means fairer odds for the bettor.

The margin calculator reveals the bookmaker's built-in edge on any market. Enter the odds for every outcome and this bookmaker margin calculator sums the implied probabilities, calculates the overround, and shows the true probability and fair odds for each selection.

Knowing a market's margin tells you how competitive a sportsbook's pricing really is. Our overround calculator helps you compare books and steer your action toward the sharpest, lowest-margin lines.

Why Use Our Margin Calculator?

Lower margins mean better long-term value, but the headline price rarely reveals the hold. This calculator exposes the overround so you can avoid high-margin markets, compare sportsbooks fairly, and derive the true odds behind any line.

Pro Tips for Smarter Betting

  • Lower-margin books give you more value over time — shop accordingly.
  • Margins are often higher on parlays, props and less popular markets.
  • Use the true odds output as a fair-value baseline for +EV analysis.

Frequently Asked Questions
