How Hedging Works

You placed a bet that is now in a winning position. By placing a counter-bet (hedge) on the opposite outcome at current odds, you can lock in a guaranteed profit no matter what happens. The calculator finds the optimal hedge stake so your profit is equalized across both outcomes.

Hedge Stake = (Original Payout) / (Hedge Decimal Odds) — this ensures the hedge payout equals the original payout.

The hedging calculator shows you the exact counter-bet needed to lock in profit or cut losses on an existing wager. Enter your original stake and odds, plus the current price on the opposite outcome, and this hedge bet calculator returns the optimal hedge stake and your profit on either result.

Hedging is essential for futures, live betting and parlays where the situation has changed since you placed your original bet. This lock-in profit bet calculator guarantees you walk away ahead no matter what happens.

The Hedging Formula Explained

Hedging means placing a bet on the opposite outcome to lock in profit or cut a loss. This hedging calculator works out exactly how much to stake on the other side so your result is the same whichever way the event goes.

Formula & Worked Example

The hedge stake is Original Potential Return / Opposing Decimal Odds. Say you hold a $100 futures ticket at 5.00 (potential return $500) and the other side is now available at 1.50. Hedge stake = 500 / 1.50 = $333.33. After laying out $100 + $333.33 = $433.33, either result returns $500, locking in a guaranteed $66.67 profit.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Hedging too early and sacrificing most of the edge you originally found.
  • Forgetting the hedge stake is extra money at risk until the event settles.
  • Ignoring the vig on the opposing side, which shrinks the locked profit.
  • Rounding the hedge stake so the outcomes no longer balance.
