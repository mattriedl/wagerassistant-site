How a Lucky 63 Bet Works

Formula & Worked Example

The 63 bets are 6 + 15 + 20 + 15 + 6 + 1. At $1 a line that is $63 total. If all six win at 2.0 each the return is 6x2 + 15x4 + 20x8 + 15x16 + 6x32 + 1x64 = 12 + 60 + 160 + 240 + 192 + 64 = $728. The large number of lines means even two or three winners can return a meaningful amount.

Common Mistakes to Avoid
