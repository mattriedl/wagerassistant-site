About Wager Assistant

Wager Assistant is a free collection of betting calculators and plain-English guides. The goal is simple: help bettors see the real math behind a bet — payouts, true odds, the bookmaker’s margin and risk — before they place it.

What we offer

The site has 32 calculators covering the bets people actually place: straight bets and parlays, round robins and teasers for US sportsbooks, and Lucky 15s, Yankees and each-way bets for UK and Irish bookmakers. Analytical tools such as the no-vig calculator, expected value calculator and Kelly criterion calculator help you judge whether a price is fair and how much to stake. The blog explains the strategy and the math behind them.

How the calculators work

  • They run in your browser. Every calculation happens on your own device. The odds and stakes you type are never sent to us or stored.
  • They show their working. Each calculator page explains the formula it uses, so you can check the result yourself.
  • They handle every odds format. American (+150 / −110), decimal (2.50) and fractional (6/4) odds are all supported where they apply.
  • No sign-up, no paywall. Every tool is free to use with no account.

How we check our math

Each calculator uses the standard, published formula for its bet type, and the formula is explained on the calculator’s page. For example, a parlay’s combined decimal odds are the product of each leg’s decimal odds, and a no-vig price comes from dividing each side’s implied probability by the total of all sides. Many pages include worked examples you can reproduce yourself to confirm the result. If you find a result that looks wrong, please tell us — we correct confirmed errors quickly.

Independence

Wager Assistant is independent. We are not a sportsbook, we do not take bets, and we do not offer tips or picks. The site does not use sportsbook affiliate links, so no bookmaker pays us to recommend it. The site is funded by banner advertising, which is clearly marked as advertising and kept separate from the calculators and guides.

Responsible betting

Calculators can show you the true cost of a bet, but they cannot make a losing bet a winning one. You must be of legal gambling age where you live (21+ in most US states, 18+ in the UK). Please read our responsible gambling page for tools and free, confidential support.
