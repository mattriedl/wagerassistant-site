The Accumulator Odds Formula Explained

An accumulator (acca) rolls several selections into one bet where every leg must win and the winnings ride onto the next. This accumulator calculator multiplies all the decimal odds together to show the combined price and total payout.

Formula & Worked Example

The formula is Combined Odds = Leg1 x Leg2 x ... x LegN. A four-fold where every leg is 1.50 gives 1.5 x 1.5 x 1.5 x 1.5 = 1.5^4 = 5.0625. A $10 stake returns 10 x 5.0625 = $50.63, a profit of $40.63. Add a fifth leg at 1.50 and the odds jump to 7.59 - the exponential growth that makes accas so popular.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Adding weak legs that drag down the real value of the acca.
  • Forgetting a single losing leg voids the entire bet.
  • Ignoring acca insurance or bonus offers that change the value.
  • Overloading legs so the win probability becomes tiny.
