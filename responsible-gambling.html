Responsible Gambling

Betting should be entertainment, not a way to make money or escape problems. Our calculators are built to show the real cost of a bet — including the bookmaker’s built-in edge — so you can make informed choices. If gambling has stopped being fun, free and confidential help is available right now.

Get help now

United States

National Problem Gambling Helpline
Call or text 1-800-MY-RESET (1-800-697-3738), or chat online. Free, confidential, 24/7. Run by the National Council on Problem Gambling.

In a crisis, call or text 988 or call 911.

United Kingdom

National Gambling Helpline (GamCare)
Call free on 0808 8020 133, 24 hours a day, or chat at gamcare.org.uk.

Block UK-licensed gambling sites with GAMSTOP. Talk to Samaritans any time on 116 123.

Peer support is available through Gamblers Anonymous, and families can find support through Gam-Anon.

Know the legal age

You must be of legal gambling age where you live. In most US states that is 21; in the UK it is 18. Laws vary, so check the rules in your state or country before you bet.

Warning signs

Gambling may be becoming a problem if you:

  • bet more than you planned, or more than you can afford to lose;
  • chase losses by betting again to win back money you’ve lost;
  • borrow money, sell things or miss bills to gamble;
  • feel restless or irritable when you try to cut back;
  • hide your gambling from family or friends, or lie about it;
  • gamble to escape stress, worry or low mood.

If any of these sound familiar, the helplines above are free and confidential, and you don’t need to be in crisis to call.

Tools to stay in control

  • Set a budget before you start and treat it as the cost of entertainment. Never bet money you need for bills.
  • Use your sportsbook’s limits. Licensed operators let you set deposit, loss, wager and time limits, take a break, or self-exclude.
  • Self-exclude across operators with GAMSTOP in the UK, or through your state’s self-exclusion program in the US.
  • Block gambling payments. Many banks let you block gambling transactions on your card.
  • Keep a record of every bet. Our ROI calculator shows honestly whether you are winning or losing over time.

Understand the house edge

Sportsbooks build a margin into every price, so most bettors lose over time. Our no-vig calculator and margin calculator show how big that edge is, and the parlay calculator shows how quickly it grows when you combine bets. No calculator, system or staking plan can guarantee profit — be especially wary of progression systems such as the Martingale, which raise your stakes after losses.
