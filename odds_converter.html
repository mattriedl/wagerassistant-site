Understanding Odds Formats

American: +150 means $150 profit on a $100 bet. −110 means you must bet $110 to win $100.
Decimal: 2.50 means a $1 bet returns $2.50 total (including stake).
Fractional: 3/2 means $3 profit for every $2 staked.
Implied Probability: The percentage chance of winning that the odds represent.

Pro Tips for Smarter Betting

  • Decimal odds are the easiest to compare and to multiply for parlays.
  • Implied probability reveals the bookmaker's margin — add up both sides of a market to see the overround.
  • Fractional odds are common in the UK and horse racing; decimal dominates Europe; American is standard in the US.

Frequently Asked Questions

For positive odds: decimal = (odds ÷ 100) + 1. For negative odds: decimal = (100 ÷ |odds|) + 1. Our odds converter does this instantly and in both directions.
Implied probability = 1 ÷ decimal odds × 100%. It tells you the win chance the price reflects, which you can compare with your own estimate to find value.
Subtract 1 from the decimal odds to get the profit per unit, then express it as a fraction. For example, 2.50 becomes 3/2. The converter handles this automatically.
