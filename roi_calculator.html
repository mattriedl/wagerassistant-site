Understanding Betting ROI

ROI = (Net Profit / Total Wagered) × 100%. A positive ROI means you're profitable. Win Rate = Bets Won / Total Bets. Yield is the profit per unit staked — a yield of 5% means you earn 5 cents per dollar wagered on average. Professional bettors typically aim for 2–10% yield over large samples.

The Betting ROI Formula Explained

Return on investment (ROI), also called yield, measures how efficiently your betting turns stakes into profit. This betting ROI calculator divides your net profit by your total amount staked to give a single percentage you can track over time.

Formula & Worked Example

The formula is ROI = (Net Profit / Total Staked) x 100. If you placed 200 bets averaging $25 (total staked $5000) and finished $400 ahead, your ROI is (400 / 5000) x 100 = 8%. A sustained 5-10% ROI over hundreds of bets is strong; anything above 10% long-term is elite and rare.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Judging ROI over too few bets, where variance dominates.
  • Confusing ROI (profit per stake) with bankroll growth rate.
  • Counting only winning bets in the total staked.
  • Chasing high ROI with huge stakes instead of consistent, sized bets.
