How Asian Handicaps Work

Whole line (-1, +2): Win, lose, or push (stake refunded).
Half line (-0.5, +1.5): Win or lose — no push possible.
Quarter line (-0.75, +1.25): Split into two equal bets on adjacent lines. One half may win while the other pushes or loses.

Pro Tips for Smarter Betting

  • Quarter lines (e.g. −0.25) split your stake between the two nearest half-lines.
  • A push on a whole-number handicap refunds your stake.
  • Asian handicaps usually carry lower margins than 1X2 markets — better long-term value.

How Asian Handicap Settlement Works

Asian handicaps remove the draw by giving one team a goal (or part-goal) head start. This asian handicap calculator applies the handicap to the final score and settles whole, half and quarter lines - including the split-stake quarter handicaps that can win or lose half.

Formula & Worked Example

Quarter lines such as -0.75 split your stake across two handicaps (-0.5 and -1.0). Back a team at -0.75 for $100 at decimal 1.90 and suppose they win by exactly one goal: the -0.5 half wins in full (returns $190) while the -1.0 half is a push (stake returned). Net result is a $45 profit instead of the full $90. Win by two or more and both halves land for the full payout.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Misreading quarter lines like -0.25 and -0.75, which split the stake in two.
  • Forgetting that the underdog's +handicap mirrors the favorite's -handicap.
  • Assuming a push loses - a tied handicap returns your stake.
  • Confusing Asian totals (goal lines) with Asian handicaps on the result.
