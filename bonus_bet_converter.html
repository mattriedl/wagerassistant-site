How Bonus Bet Conversion Works

Free Bet (SNR): You place the free bet as a "back" bet and lay against it on an exchange. Since the stake isn't returned, you only receive the profit portion if it wins. The lay bet ensures you profit either way.

Optimal Lay Stake (SNR): = (Back Odds − 1) × Free Bet ÷ (Lay Odds − Commission)
Free Bet (SR): Stake is returned if you win, so conversion rate is higher.
Risk-Free: You get refunded if the bet loses, so you can be more aggressive with higher odds.

Pro Tips for Smarter Betting

  • Higher back odds generally convert free bets at a better rate.
  • Aim to keep back and lay odds close to minimise the lay liability.
  • Typical free-bet conversion rates range from 70-80% of face value.
