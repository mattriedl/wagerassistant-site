How Bonus Bet Conversion Works

Free Bet (SNR): You place the free bet as a "back" bet and lay against it on an exchange. Since the stake isn't returned, you only receive the profit portion if it wins. The lay bet ensures you profit either way.

Optimal Lay Stake (SNR): = (Back Odds − 1) × Free Bet ÷ (Lay Odds − Commission)
Free Bet (SR): Stake is returned if you win, so conversion rate is higher.
Risk-Free: You get refunded if the bet loses, so you can be more aggressive with higher odds.

Pro Tips for Smarter Betting

  • Higher back odds generally convert free bets at a better rate.
  • Aim to keep back and lay odds close to minimise the lay liability.
  • Typical free-bet conversion rates range from 70-80% of face value.

How to Value a Bonus Bet

A bonus bet (or free bet) only pays out the winnings, not the stake, so it is worth less than its face value. This bonus bet calculator estimates the real cash value based on the odds you use it on and your conversion rate.

Formula & Worked Example

Expected cash value is roughly Bonus Amount x (Decimal Odds - 1) x Win Probability. A $50 free bet placed at 3.00 returns $100 profit if it wins; at a fair 33.3% win chance the expected value is 50 x 2.00 x 0.333 = $33.30. Using the matched-betting method of laying the other side typically converts a free bet to 70-80% of face value - about $35-$40 here.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using a free bet at very low odds, which wastes most of its value.
  • Forgetting the stake is not returned on a winning bonus bet.
  • Chasing huge-odds longshots that rarely land.
  • Ignoring wagering or rollover conditions attached to the bonus.
