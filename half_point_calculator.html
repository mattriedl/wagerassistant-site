Understanding Buying & Selling Points

Most sportsbooks allow you to buy or sell points to adjust a spread or total in your favor (or against you):

  • Buying Points: Move the line in your favor (e.g., -3.5 → -2.5) at worse odds. Costs you juice.
  • Selling Points: Move the line against you (e.g., -3.5 → -4.5) for better odds. Earns you juice.

Key Numbers: In NFL betting, the numbers 3 and 7 are especially important because many games are decided by these margins (field goals and touchdowns). Buying through key numbers (e.g., from -3.5 to -2.5) is often worth the extra cost.

Standard Pricing: Typically, each half-point costs 10 cents of juice (e.g., -110 → -120). Crossing key numbers like 3 often costs 15–25 cents extra.

Rule of Thumb: If the implied probability shift per point is less than 3%, buying may not be worth the cost.

The half-point calculator shows whether buying or selling points on a spread or total is worth the change in price. Enter the original line and odds and the line you want to move to, and this buying points calculator reveals the break-even cost and whether the new number offers value.

Why Use Our Half-Point Calculator?

Sportsbooks charge more to move across key numbers because those margins occur most often. This calculator tells you whether the price you are paying for a half-point is justified by the added win probability, helping you avoid overpaying.

Pro Tips for Smarter Betting

  • Buying onto or off key numbers (3, 7 in the NFL) carries the most value.
  • Buying a half-point on totals near common scores can be worthwhile.
  • Compare the extra cost with the realistic gain in win probability before buying.
