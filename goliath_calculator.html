How a Goliath Bet Works

A Goliath is the largest common full-cover bet: eight selections across 247 bets, with no singles. This goliath calculator settles every double through to the eight-fold so you can track returns across all 247 lines.

Formula & Worked Example

The 247 bets are 28 doubles + 56 trebles + 70 four-folds + 56 five-folds + 28 six-folds + 8 seven-folds + 1 eight-fold = 247. At $0.50 a line that is $123.50 total. Because there are no singles, at least two of the eight selections must win to return anything - but with so many combinations, four or five winners can still produce a large payout.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Underestimating the huge 247-unit stake (494 units each-way).
  • Expecting a return from a single winning selection (there are no singles).
  • Confusing a Goliath with a Lucky 63, which uses six selections plus singles.
  • Staking a full unit per line and risking far more than intended.
