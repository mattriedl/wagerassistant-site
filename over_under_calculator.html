Understanding Over/Under (Totals) Bets

The sportsbook sets a total line for a game (e.g., 45.5 total points). You bet on whether the combined final score goes Over or Under that line.

Half-point lines (e.g., 45.5) prevent pushes. Whole-number lines (e.g., 45) can result in a push (stake returned) if the total lands exactly on the line. Standard odds are typically −110 on both sides.

The over/under calculator settles totals bets in an instant. Enter the total line, the odds and your stake to see your payout, profit and whether the result lands over, under or on the number (a push). It works for points, runs, goals and any combined-total market.

Totals betting is one of the most popular markets in sports because it does not depend on which side wins. Our totals betting calculator helps you understand the value in each price and plan your stakes with confidence.

Pro Tips for Smarter Betting

  • Half-point totals (e.g. 47.5) eliminate the chance of a push.
  • Weather, pace and injuries move totals — shop lines as news breaks.
  • Compare the implied probability of the over and under to spot juice imbalances.

How Over/Under Betting Works

An over/under (totals) bet is a wager on the combined score of both teams versus a line set by the book. This over under calculator settles the bet against the final total and prices your payout at the quoted odds.

Formula & Worked Example

Totals are typically priced around -110 (1.9091). If the line is 45.5 and you take the over, a combined 46 or more points wins. A $55 stake at -110 returns 55 x 1.9091 = $105, a profit of $50. A whole-number total like 45 can push if the teams combine for exactly 45, returning your stake.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Forgetting overtime points count toward most totals.
  • Ignoring the half-point hook that turns a push into a win or loss.
  • Assuming weather and pace do not move totals - they do, heavily.
  • Comparing totals between books without matching the exact number.
