How Patent Bets Work

A Patent is a full-coverage combination bet with 7 bets across 3 selections: 3 singles, 3 doubles, and 1 treble. Because it includes singles, you only need one selection to win for a return. A Patent is essentially a Lucky 15 with fewer selections — it provides safety through singles while still offering solid returns through doubles and the treble. It is the smallest "full coverage" bet type, making it affordable and popular with cautious bettors.

How a Patent Bet Works

A Patent is a full-cover bet on three selections across seven bets: three singles, three doubles and one treble. This patent calculator settles all seven lines, so even a single winning selection returns money.

Formula & Worked Example

The seven bets are A, B, C (singles), AB, AC, BC (doubles) and ABC (treble). At $1 a line that is $7 total. If all three win at 2.0 each, the singles return 3 x 2 = $6, the doubles 3 x 4 = $12 and the treble 1 x 8 = $8, for a total of $26. Unlike a Trixie, one winner alone still pays out via its single.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Confusing a Patent with a Trixie, which has no singles.
  • Forgetting the 7-unit total stake per Patent.
  • Expecting Trixie-level returns despite the extra single stakes.
  • Applying each-way terms and doubling the stake unexpectedly.
