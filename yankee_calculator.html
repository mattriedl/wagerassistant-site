How Yankee Bets Work

A Yankee is a combination bet with 11 bets across 4 selections: 6 doubles, 4 trebles, and 1 four-fold accumulator. Unlike a Lucky 15, a Yankee does not include singles, making it cheaper but requiring at least 2 selections to win for a return. The Yankee offers higher potential returns per unit stake because it focuses on multi-selection parlays. It is a popular bet type in horse racing and football.

How a Yankee Bet Works

A Yankee is a full-cover bet on four selections totalling 11 bets: six doubles, four trebles and one four-fold. This yankee calculator settles every line so partial winners still produce a return.

Formula & Worked Example

From 4 selections you get 6 doubles + 4 trebles + 1 fourfold = 11 bets. At $1 per line that is $11 total. If all four win at 2.0 each, the doubles return 6 x (2^2) = $24, the trebles 4 x (2^3) = $32 and the fourfold 1 x (2^4) = $16, for a total of $72 from an $11 outlay. At least two selections must win for any return.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Expecting a payout when only one selection wins.
  • Forgetting the 11-unit stake (22 units each-way).
  • Confusing a Yankee with a Lucky 15, which adds four singles.
  • Miscounting the combinations when adding or removing a selection.
