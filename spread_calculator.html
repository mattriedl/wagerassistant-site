Understanding Point Spreads

The point spread is a handicap applied to the favored team to level the playing field. −3.5 means the favorite must win by 4+ points. +7 means the underdog can lose by up to 6 points and still "cover." Standard spread odds are −110 on both sides (the bookmaker's vig). The break-even win percentage at −110 odds is approximately 52.38%.

A point spread calculator helps you settle against-the-spread (ATS) wagers in seconds. Enter the spread, the price and your stake to see your potential payout, profit and break-even point — perfect for NFL, NBA, college football and any sport where the margin of victory decides the bet.

Spread betting levels the playing field between mismatched teams by giving the underdog a head start. Our spread bet payout calculator shows you exactly what a covered spread returns so you can shop for the best number and the best price.

How Point Spread Betting Works

A point spread handicaps the favorite and gives the underdog a head start, creating a near 50/50 market. This spread calculator applies the spread to the final score and prices the bet so you can see your payout before kickoff.

Formula & Worked Example

Spreads are usually priced around -110 (decimal 1.9091). Back a favorite at -6.5 and they must win by 7 or more to cover. On a $110 stake at -110, a cover returns 110 x 1.9091 = $210, a profit of $100. A whole-number spread like -7 can push (win by exactly 7), returning your stake, while the -6.5 hook removes the tie.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Overlooking the hook - a half point is the difference between a push and a loss.
  • Ignoring key numbers (3 and 7 in the NFL) when comparing spreads.
  • Assuming -110 is standard when some books charge -115 or more.
  • Confusing the spread result with the moneyline winner.

Frequently Asked Questions

Covering the spread means your team beats the point spread. A −3.5 favorite must win by 4 or more; a +3.5 underdog can lose by 3 or fewer (or win outright) and still cover.
Convert the spread odds (usually −110) to decimal and multiply by your stake. Our spread calculator does this instantly and shows your profit and break-even rate.
