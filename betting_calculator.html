How It Works

The Bet Payout Formula Explained

Every single-bet payout comes down to one calculation: your stake multiplied by the decimal odds. This bet payout calculator converts American and fractional prices to decimals first, then applies the formula so you always see the full return and the profit on top of your stake.

Formula & Worked Example

The core formula is Total Return = Stake x Decimal Odds and Profit = Total Return - Stake. Suppose you back a team at American odds of -110. That converts to decimal odds of 1.9091. On a $50 stake the math is 50 x 1.9091 = $95.45 total return, leaving a profit of $45.45. Switch the price to +150 (decimal 2.50) and the same $50 returns $125 for a $75 profit.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Confusing total return with profit - the payout figure already includes your original stake.
  • Forgetting to convert American or fractional odds to decimal before multiplying.
  • Rounding decimal odds too early, which distorts the payout on large stakes.
  • Ignoring that favorites (negative odds) risk more to win less.
